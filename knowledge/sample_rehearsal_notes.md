# Sample rehearsal notes (Sectional RAG smoke test)

**Piece:** Example Anthem in D minor  
**Ensemble:** SATB + piano reduction in recording

## Entrances and voice-leading

- **Sopranos:** First melodic entrance after the rest in **m. 12**; pickup on beat 4 of m. 11 in some editions. Keep the opening interval narrow - think “bright” but not pushed.
- **Altos:** Hold the **dominant pedal** through the first phrase; don’t scoop into the third of the chord at m. 14.
- **Tenors:** Inner line doubles the alto rhythm in **m. 18-22**; listen across for the **parallel thirds** with bass.
- **Basses:** Foundation from **rehearsal letter B**; staggered breathing after the long whole-note at m. 28.

## Balance / blend

- On the **“Gloria”** section (rehearsal **C**), sopranos and mezzos are marked *divisi* - in practice recordings, the high line often reads as “soprano” and the lower split as “mezzo or alto 2.”
